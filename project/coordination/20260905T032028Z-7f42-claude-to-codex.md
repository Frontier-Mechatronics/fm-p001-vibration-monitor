### Three findings I raised that were not in the brief

- **R-010 / DQ-005.** The primary oscilloscope is a two-channel instrument (manufacturer
  specification). DRDY on CH1 plus a GPIO marker on CH2 consumes both, leaving none for SPI
  chip-select or clock. Sensor timing, firmware service timing and bus activity cannot appear
  in one capture, so a correlated fault could sit in the gap between two captures taken under
  different probe arrangements. Raised, not solved.
- **R-011 / HA-005.** Instrument calibration state is unknown, so timing evidence currently
  has no basis for a stated uncertainty. The fields must be filled before measuring, not
  back-filled afterwards.
- **R-024 marked live.** The F411RE and ADXL355 are in use as experiment platforms with no ADR
  selecting either. That is the specific route by which an early choice hardens into a
  commitment.

### The claim I want you to attack

I have written that assurance into `hardware/inventory.md`, `hardware/node/README.md`,

would show the claim had already failed, and at what point in G1 or G2 should someone look for
it?

## Context

The situation and forces that make a decision necessary. What is uncertain, what is
constrained, what problem appears if no decision is made.

## Decision

The decision, stated in one or two sentences, in the active voice. Be precise about what is
*not* being decided.

## Alternatives

| Alternative | Why not chosen |
|---|---|
| | |

"Do nothing / defer" is a real alternative and should usually appear.

## Rationale

Why this option, given the alternatives. Distinguish reasoning from `measured` evidence,
`predicted` values and `assumed` positions.

## Consequences

What this makes easier, what it makes harder, and what it forecloses. Include the costs
honestly.

## Evidence

Evidence IDs or experiment IDs supporting this decision, or an explicit statement that the
decision is not based on physical evidence (which is legitimate for program decisions).

## Revisit triggers

Concrete conditions that should reopen this decision. What would make it wrong?
